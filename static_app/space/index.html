How it works

1. The input is normalized like the training corpus (e.g. ሐ/ኀ→ሀ, ሠ→ሰ, ዐ→አ, ፀ→ጸ; numbers → <NUM>) and only the sentence being written is used.
2. The final one or two words form the context (sentence start is padded with <s>).
3. Candidate next words and their Kneser-Ney probabilities are looked up in tables precomputed from a 4-gram model trained on ~60 M words of the GPAC corpus (deduplicated).
4. If the two-word context was not frequent enough to be stored, the app backs off to the one-word context, then to word frequency; backed-off scores are marked with ~.
5. The top five are shown; ። means the model expects the sentence to end. With a partly typed word, only words starting with those letters are suggested.
